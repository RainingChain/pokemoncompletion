## Pokémon Emerald: 100% Completion Guide

How to catch and EV train the recommended Pokémon to complete 100% Emerald:

- Catching and training Pokémon
    - Swampert (for Battle Frontier)
    - Metagross (for Battle Frontier)
    - Latios (for Battle Frontier)
    - Kecleon (for Liechi Berry in Mirage Island)
    - Smeargle (for Trainer Hill)
- Battle Frontier

### Smeargle

[Video guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6-sss9giRHo)

- Catch Smeargle and breed until it has 31 Speed IV and +Speed nature (ex: Jolly, Timid)
    - Catch a Female Smeargle or Ditto with the correct nature, and give it an Everstone when breeding.
    - Have a Pokémon with ability Magma Armour or Flame Body in the first slot to hatch eggs faster.
- Get a Gyarados with Dragon Rage (Level 25). Battle Ditto. Use Dragon Rage. Ditto uses Dragon Rage. Put Ditto to Sleep. Switch to Smeargle. Sketch Dragon Rage.
- Give Smeargle x10 Carbos
- Give Smeargle Pokérus (not needed, but without it, resulting Smeargle will only have 17 Speed)
- Give Smeargle a Macro Brace. Get x5 other Pokémon with Exp. Share (not needed, but without it, resulting Smeargle will only have 17 Speed).
- In Route 103, battle level 2 Wingull. During the battle, switch to all other 5 Pokémon. This ensures Smearlge earns only 1 exp per battle.
- Repeat until Smeargle is about to raise to level 6.
- Level 5 Smeargle should have 18 Speed.
- Repeat all the same steps to have x2 Level 5 Smeargle with 18 Speed and Dragon Rage.

### Battle Tower

Objective:

- 100 consecutive Single wins (Level 50 or Open Level) for Gold Trophy
- 56 consecutive Single wins (Level 50) for Winning Ribbon
- 56 consecutive Single wins (Open Level) for Victory Ribbon

Team:

- Latios @ Lum Berry: Calm Mind, Psychic, Dragon Claw, Thunderbolt
- Swampert @ Leftovers: Earthquake, Surf, Ice Beam, Counter
- Metagross @ Choice Band: Meteor Mash, Earthquake, Shadow Ball, Explosion
