## Pokémon Pinball Ruby & Sapphire: Complete Pokédex

### Summary

On April 8th 2024, RainingChain became the first player to complete the Pokédex on Pinball Ruby & Sapphire US (and to document it).

### How To

To obtain Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile, Aerodactyl:

- On Pokémon Pinball R&S JP, use the GET Special Guests Card e-Reader card to catch all 4 Pokémons.
- Link the Pokédex of Pokémon Pinball R&S JP and Pokémon Pinball R&S US.
- Catch all 4 Pokémons in Pokémon Pinball R&S US.
- For more info, check this [video guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kbVM6YqX0Yw&ab_channel=RacingChu)

"GET Special Guests e-Reader Card (09-A002) (JP)" is one of the rarest e-Reader cards. The reason for their rarity is that they are demo cards that were placed in selected stores in Japan throughout September 2003 and were not for distribution to customers.
